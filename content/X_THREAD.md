### Tweet 3: The Secret Weapon: Kelly Criterion Math 📐
Don't guess your bet size. Professional sharp traders use the Kelly Criterion:

$$f^* = \frac{p(b + 1) - 1}{b}$$

If Panta has YES at $0.40 (implied 40%), but your research shows a 60% true probability:
- Net odds $b = (1 - 0.40) / 0.40 = 1.50$
- Optimal Half-Kelly Bet = 16.7% of bankroll
- Expected Value = +$0.20 per $1 risked (+50% ROI)
